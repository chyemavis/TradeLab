Practice Portfolio

A practice trading app with fake money for complete investing beginners, with confusing terms explained in plain English and a mini animation.

Scope said: "tap a term, see text plus a visual aid." Now we know exactly what that means: five specific terms, each with its own mini animation, tappable wherever they appear.

The core journey

Screens, behavior & criteria

Intro & guided tutorial
  • A short intro to the app appears on first open
  • It ends with the option to start the guided tutorial or skip it
  • The tutorial walks through one buy and one sell on the real screens
  • It isn't shown again once finished or skipped
Home & portfolio
  • Fake cash balance shown prominently at the top
  • Each holding shows stock, shares, current value, and gain/loss in $ and %
  • Values update as prices move
  • Empty state: "No stocks yet," pointing to browsing
Stock detail & prices
  • Current price and a price chart
  • Prices move over time in a realistic way
  • Buy / Sell buttons
Term explanations THE KERNEL
market capP/E ratiovolatilitydividendunrealized gain/loss
  • Tappable wherever they appear
  • Plain-English explanation plus a mini animation
  • Each term gets its own animation, not one generic animation
Buying & selling
  • Buy with enough cash: cash goes down and the holding appears or grows
  • Sell shares you own: cash goes up and the holding shrinks or disappears
  • Buying more than the cash covers → blocked with an error
  • Selling more shares than owned → blocked with an error
Top up fake money
  • The user can add fake cash to their balance

States

First use

The intro appears. The cash balance is $10,000 (assumption) and there are no holdings.

Empty portfolio

"No stocks yet," pointing to browsing.

Invalid trade

An error message appears and nothing changes.

Coming back

Cash, holdings, and the intro-seen flag persist.

Look & feel

Minimal and dark, like a real trading app. Green for gains and red for losses (assumption). The mini animations are the design highlight.
