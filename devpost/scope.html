Practice Portfolio

A practice trading app with fake money that shows beginners what investing terms mean, visually and in plain English.

The unique kernel

Investing terms and charts are explained through a mix of text and visual aids, not just text definitions. You tap a confusing term and see what it means, while you're trading.

How it fits together

The essentials

Who it's for

A complete beginner who wants to invest but doesn't know "what is good or bad" or where to find information. Today they'd just read about it, with no safe place to try.

Look & feel

Like a real trading app, not a toy. No specific reference app yet.

Why it matters

The learner wants to start investing and to learn through building it. It's also practice for getting better design output from AI.

"Working" means

Go through the tutorial, buy and sell with fake money, see the portfolio, and tap a term for a visual explanation (the "oh, that's cool" moment).

Proof-of-concept boundary

Now
  • Short intro tutorial
  • Buy and sell with a fake cash balance
  • Portfolio view
  • Tap-to-explain for a small set of terms (text + visual aid)
Later
  • More creative or interactive ways of showing what terms mean
  • Explanations for more terms
Cut
  • Real money and brokerage connections: it's a practice tool; real money adds risk and complexity without helping prove the idea.
  • Accounts and login: not needed for a 1-minute demo.
Open for later phases
  • Real stock prices (from an API) or simulated ones?
  • Which terms get explanations in the proof of concept?
